# Maintainers Wanted

HiFiBerry OS relies on community maintainers to keep its player integrations working well across new software versions and diverse hardware configurations. We are currently looking for maintainers for the following players:

- **Spotify** (librespot)
- **Airplay** (shairport-sync)
- **Squeezelite** (LMS client)

## What does a maintainer do?

As a player maintainer you:

- **Test new upstream versions** — when a new release of librespot, shairport-sync, or squeezelite comes out, you test it against the current HiFiBerry OS and report issues or prepare updated packages.
- **Test with different hardware** — you verify that the player works correctly across a range of HiFiBerry sound cards (DAC+, DAC2, Beocreate, etc.) and flag regressions.
- **Create and update packages** — you build new Debian packages, update changelogs and version numbers, and coordinate with the HiFiBerry team to publish them.
- **Stay close to the upstream project** — you follow the upstream issue tracker, mailing lists, or release notes so you can react quickly when something changes.

## What this means in practice

Maintaining a player requires that you actively use the service you are responsible for:

- **Spotify maintainer** — you need an active Spotify Premium subscription and regularly stream music through librespot on HiFiBerry hardware.
- **Airplay maintainer** — you need Apple hardware (iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Apple TV) to test AirPlay 1 and AirPlay 2 streaming and multi-room scenarios.
- **Squeezelite maintainer** — you need a running Logitech Media Server (LMS) instance and regularly use it to play music through squeezelite on HiFiBerry hardware.

## What we provide

During the time you are actively working as a maintainer, HiFiBerry will provide you with multiple sound cards on loan so you have the hardware needed to test properly.

## How to get involved

Send us an email at [support@hifiberry.com](mailto:support@hifiberry.com) and tell us:

- Which player you would like to maintain
- Your experience with the player and with packaging (Debian, Raspberry Pi, etc.)
- How you plan to use and test the player regularly

We look forward to working with you!
